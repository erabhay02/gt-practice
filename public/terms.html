Who can use ThinkSprout

Accounts are for parents and legal guardians who are 18 or older. You may let your children use ThinkSprout under your account and supervision. During the invite-only beta, an invite code is required to create an account.

What ThinkSprout is (and isn't)

ThinkSprout offers original practice questions in the style of cognitive abilities tests for young children. Scores in the app are practice percentages, not official test scores, and practicing does not guarantee any result on a real test or admission to any program. ThinkSprout is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Riverside Insights. CogAT® is a registered trademark of Riverside Assessments, LLC.
