🌱 ThinkSprout Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

ThinkSprout is a practice app for children in Kindergarten through 4th grade who are preparing for gifted & talented screening tests. It is available on the web and as an app for iPhone, iPad and Android. ThinkSprout is designed to collect as little information as possible. Accounts are created and managed by a parent or legal guardian, never by children.

What we collect

Everything below is provided by the parent, or created when a child practices under the parent's account.

InformationWhy
Parent's email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash)To sign in, send sign-up and password-reset codes, and keep your family's progress in sync across your devices.
Each child's first name or nickname, grade (Kindergarten through 4th), and the buddy picture you pickTo show questions for the right level and say hello. Please use a first name or nickname only.
Test date (optional)To show a countdown to test day.
Practice results: which question types were practiced, how many were answered correctly, which questions were recently shown, and whenTo adjust difficulty, avoid repeating questions, build each day's practice, and show you progress.
Invite code and the date you agreed to this policyTo run the invite-only beta and record your consent.

We do not collect children's full names, photos, voices, locations, contacts, or advertising or device identifiers. ThinkSprout does not use the microphone or camera. It shows no ads, has no in-app chat or sharing, and includes no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Read-aloud

Questions are read aloud from recordings that are built into ThinkSprout, so nothing your child does is sent anywhere to produce speech. If a recording is missing, the device's own built-in voice is used instead.

Where information is stored

Practice works offline: results are saved on your device and synced to your account when you're online. Your account data is stored in the United States with these service providers, who process it only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: sign-in and the database that holds your account, children and practice results.
  • Twilio SendGrid: sends the sign-up and password-reset code emails (it receives your email address).
  • Cloudflare and GitHub: host the ThinkSprout website. Like any website host, they handle standard technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages securely.

If you install ThinkSprout from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google handles the download under their own privacy policies. We do not add any of their analytics or advertising tools to the app.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers above, or when required by law.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each family's data is walled off at the database level, so one account can never read another family's children or results. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

ThinkSprout is used by children under 13 under a parent's account. A parent or guardian creates the account, confirms they are 18 or older, and agrees to this policy before any child information is saved. Children cannot create accounts, make purchases, or change settings; those areas are behind a grown-ups-only check. As a parent you can, at any time:

  • Review your children's information and practice history in Grown-ups → Progress and Grown-ups → Children.
  • Change a child's name, grade, buddy picture or test date in Grown-ups → Children.
  • Delete a child's practice history (Reset progress), or remove a child, in Grown-ups → Children.
  • Stop all collection and delete everything by deleting your account in Grown-ups → Account → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and applies to every device.

You can also email us to make any of these requests.

Signing out

Signing out removes your family's data from that device; it stays in your account until you delete it.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, children, and practice history are deleted from our database. When you remove a child or reset progress, that information is deleted from our database the next time the device syncs.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and, for significant changes, tell you by email or in the app before the change applies.
