🌱 ThinkSprout Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

ThinkSprout is a practice app for young children preparing for gifted & talented screening tests. It is designed to collect as little information as possible. Accounts are created and managed by a parent or legal guardian, never by children.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Parent's email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash)To sign in and keep your family's progress in sync across your devices.
Each child's first name or nickname, grade (1st or 2nd), and the buddy picture you pickTo show the right questions and say hello. Please use a first name or nickname only.
Practice results: which question types were practiced, how many were answered correctly, and whenTo adjust difficulty, avoid repeating questions, and show you progress.
Invite code and the date you agreed to this policyTo run the invite-only beta and record your consent.

We do not collect children's full names, photos, voices, locations, contacts, or device identifiers for tracking. ThinkSprout shows no ads and includes no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

How information is stored and shared

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. Practice also works offline; results are saved on your device and synced when you're online. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except service providers needed to run the app, or when required by law.

Children's privacy

ThinkSprout is used by children under 13 under a parent's account. A parent or guardian creates the account, confirms they are 18 or older, and agrees to this policy before any child information is saved. Children cannot create accounts, make purchases, or change settings; those areas are behind a grown-ups-only check.

Your choices

  • See and change your children's names, grades and test dates in Grown-ups → Children.
  • Reset a child's practice history, or remove a child, in Grown-ups → Children.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time in Grown-ups → Account → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and applies to every device.
  • Signing out removes your family's data from that device; it stays in your account until you delete it.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, children, and practice history are deleted from our database.

Not affiliated with test publishers

ThinkSprout provides original practice questions. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Riverside Insights. CogAT® is a registered trademark of Riverside Assessments, LLC.
